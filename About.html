WAW stands for "Worry About Watersheds". We chose this name because it reflects our mission to help our local watershed, the West Fork North Branch Chicago River Watershed. You may think that this is some meaningless campaign, but we assure you it is not. Think about the water around us, the water that YOU drink on an everyday basis! This pollution that we talk about is what YOU are drinking! So please, help us in our effort to make the world, and its water a better place.
